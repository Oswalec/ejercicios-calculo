## Enunciado

Si $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{3} f = 5$, $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{5} f = 2$ y $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{5} g = 4$, calcula $\displaystyle \int_{3}^{5} f$, $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{5}(2f - 3g)$ y $\displaystyle \int_{5}^{0} g$.

## Solución

1. Aditividad: $\int_0^5 f = \int_0^3 f + \int_3^5 f$, así que $\int_3^5 f = 2 - 5 = -3$.
2. Linealidad: $\int_0^5 (2f - 3g) = 2\int_0^5 f - 3\int_0^5 g = 2(2) - 3(4) = -8$.
3. Inversión de límites: $\int_5^0 g = -\int_0^5 g = -4$.

**Resultado final:** $\displaystyle \int_{3}^{5} f = -3$, $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{5}(2f - 3g) = -8$ y $\displaystyle \int_{5}^{0} g = -4$
