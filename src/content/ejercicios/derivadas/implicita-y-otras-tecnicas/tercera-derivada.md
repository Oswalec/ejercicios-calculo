## Enunciado

Halla $f'''(x)$ si $f(x) = \operatorname{sen}(2x)$.

## Solución

1. $f'(x) = 2\cos(2x)$.
2. $f''(x) = 2 \cdot \left(-\operatorname{sen}(2x)\right) \cdot 2 = -4\operatorname{sen}(2x)$.
3. $f'''(x) = -4\cos(2x) \cdot 2 = -8\cos(2x)$.

**Resultado final:** $f'''(x) = -8\cos(2x)$
